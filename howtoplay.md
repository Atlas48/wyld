# Card Types
- There are 3 card types
- One that outlines the story of a session, and two that have effects on said story
- **Boons** add positive effects applied per session.
- **Waylays** are negative effects applied per session.
- **Stories** are what you will be playing in a session.
- Story cards have an associated XP reward.
- Story cards may add or remove Boons and Waylays.
- Some Boon cards are perpetual, meaning they return to hand after play.

# Playfield
- The playfield consists of 3 areas
	1. **Episode List** (6 cards): A list of 6 stories that will be iterated over.
	2. **Active Boons** (3 cards): The boons that will be active during the current story.
	3. **Active Waylays** (5 cards): The waylays that will be active during the current story.

# Play
- Upon chronicle start...
	- 8 story cards are dealt to each player.
- Every 6 sessions...
	- Each player takes a turn to add a single story card to the episode list.
	- The ST may choose to add their own special card to the episode list.
	- If this isn't chronicle start, a single card is dealt to each player.
- At the start of each session...
	- The players play up to 3 boon cards.
	- The ST plays up to the current episode card's XP reward in Waylay cards.
- At the end of each session...
	- All the boon cards are discarded, bar the perpetual cards, which return to the player's hand.
	- All the waylay cards are discarded. If the waylay deck is empty, the discard pile is shuffled into the deck.
