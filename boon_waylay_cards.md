# Boon and Waylay Cards
## Design Philosophy
- Cards introduce concrete elements (people, objects, situations) into the fiction
- Specific enough to prompt improv, vague enough for creative freedom
- No prerequisites - must work for any character/situation
- Must add to story and fit chronicle themes
- Broadly applicable across episode types

## Perpetual Boons
(Occupy slot for entire session)

Lucy Available
:	Lucy Arkwright is present and available this session
Lucas Available
:	Lucas Arkwright is present and available this session
Payday
:	Your character gains money.

## Instant Boons
(One-use, returns to deck after use)

Resurgance
:	An object/item/person from Act 1 has returned.
Freehold Access
:	Access to a freehold (location and NPCs)
Travelling Merchant
:	Vendor with goods appears
Helpful Stranger
:	Unknown person offers assistance
Tool Found
:	Needed equipment available
Dream Vision
:	Glimpse of something important
Mysterious Stranger
:	Someone appears to help
Benevolent Chimera
:	Helpful creature from Dreaming manifests

## Waylays
(Narrative complications deployable during session)

The Past Resurfaces
:	Something from Act 1 appears
Authority Attention
:	Police/officials take interest
Family Emergency
:	Mortal family/connections need immediate attention
Chimerical Intrusion
:	Something from the Dreaming manifests
Animal Encounter
:	Creature appears or causes problem
Stranger Takes Interest
:	Unknown person pays attention to you
Unexpected Bill
:	Expense demands immediate attention
Lost Item
:	Something important goes missing
Noticed
:	Someone notices you doing something strange.
Transport Failure
:	Movement/travel method fails
Gremlins
:	Technology fails in the most annoying way.
Small Disaster
:	Minor catastrophe occurs (fire, flood, break, spill)
