# Boon and Waylay Cards
## Midnight and the Sands of Time - Act 2

### Design Philosophy
- Cards introduce concrete elements (people, objects, situations) into the fiction
- Specific enough to prompt improv, vague enough for creative freedom
- No prerequisites - must work for any character/situation
- Must add to story and fit chronicle themes
- Broadly applicable across episode types

### Perpetual Boons
(Occupy slot for entire session)

- **Lucy Available** - Lucy Arkwright is present and available this session
- **Payday** - 
- **Lucas Available** - Lucas Arkwright is present and available this session

### Instant Boons
(One-use, returns to deck after use)

- **Childhood Artifact** - An object from 1986-1990 is in play
- **Freehold Access** - Access to a freehold (location and NPCs)
- **The Favour Owed** - Someone owes you and will help once
- **Travelling Merchant** - Vendor with goods appears
- **Message Arrives** - Important communication reaches you
- **Helpful Stranger** - Unknown person offers assistance
- **Old Photograph** - Image from the past surfaces
- **Tool Found** - Needed equipment available
- **Dream Vision** - Glimpse of something important
- **Mysterious Stranger** - Someone appears to help
- **Benevolent Chimera** - Helpful creature from Dreaming manifests

### Waylays
(Narrative complications deployable during session)

- **The Past Resurfaces** - Something from 1986-1990 appears
- **Authority Attention** - Police/officials take interest
- **Family Emergency** - Mortal family/connections need immediate attention
- **Chimerical Intrusion** - Something from the Dreaming manifests
- **Animal Encounter** - Creature appears or causes problem
- **Stranger Takes Interest** - Unknown person pays attention to you
- **Unexpected Bill** - Expense demands immediate attention
- **Lost Item** - Something important goes missing
- **Curious Child** - Kid sees or asks about fae matters
- **Memory Surfaces** - Flashback to 1986-1990 disrupts scene
- **Transport Failure** - Movement/travel method fails
- **Gremlins** - Technology fails in the most annoying way.
- **Small Disaster** - Minor catastrophe occurs (fire, flood, break, spill)
